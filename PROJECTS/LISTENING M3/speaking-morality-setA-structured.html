Speaking Cue Card 1 · copy 1

Is it ever OK to break a rule to do the right thing?

Some rules seem unfair or harmful. A person might break one to help someone in need. Is that ever justified?
Structure your discussion
  1. Open: “Let's talk about the rules we follow.”
  2. Opinion: Give your opinion.
  3. Reason: Give a reason for your opinion.
  4. Agree / disagree: Listen, then agree or disagree.
  5. Agreement: Try to reach a compromise together.
Language you can use
To open
  • Let's talk about climate change now.
  • I'd like to talk about the weather and the climate.
To give an opinion
  • In my view, ...
  • I strongly believe ...
To give a reason
  • ... because ...
  • ... because, you see, ...
To agree / disagree
  • I agree with you.
  • I completely agree.
To reach agreement
  • So, could we agree that ... ?
  • How about we agree that ... ?

Should you tell a lie to protect a friend?

Your friend did something wrong. The teacher asks you directly. Do you protect them, or tell the truth?
Structure your discussion
  1. Open: “Shall we talk about honesty?”
  2. Opinion: Give your opinion.
  3. Reason: Give a reason for your opinion.
  4. Agree / disagree: Listen, then agree or disagree.
  5. Agreement: Try to reach a compromise together.
Language you can use
To open
  • Shall we talk about what is happening to our planet?
  • What do you think about all these unusual floods?
To give an opinion
  • I think ...
  • Honestly, I think ...
To give a reason
  • The main reason is that ...
  • The reason I say that is ...
To agree / disagree
  • That's a good point, but ...
  • I disagree, because ...
To reach agreement
  • Maybe we can both ...
  • Perhaps we can settle on ...
