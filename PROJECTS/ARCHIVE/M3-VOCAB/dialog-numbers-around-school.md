### Numbers Around School

*A teacher and a student talk after class. Ms. Rivera helps Leo with his survey project about numbers. City school, after school.*

| Character | Voice notes | Key line |
|:----------|:-------------|:---------|
| **Ms. Rivera** | Warm, encouraging teacher. Clear and patient, a little formal. | "So the sum of all that homework time must be quite high." |
| **Leo** | Young, curious student. Friendly and a little unsure. | "I put them in a sequence, from smallest to largest, so the data is easier to compare." |

**Ms. Rivera:** (happy) Hi Leo. How is your numbers project going?

**Leo:** (nervous) Quite well, actually. I've been measuring things around school, and I'm starting to see some interesting patterns.

**Ms. Rivera:** (curious) Patterns? Tell me more. What have you found so far?

**Leo:** Well, the average student spends about an hour on homework every night, which is more than I expected.

**Ms. Rivera:** (surprised) Really? And roughly what percentage of students said that?

**Leo:** Around sixty percent. The amount of time surprised me too, especially on weekends.

**Ms. Rivera:** (calm) So the sum of all that homework time must be quite high. Did you calculate it?

**Leo:** Yes, I did. I also checked the probability of rain this weekend, since we're planning a school trip.

**Ms. Rivera:** (confident) Good thinking. Did you measure the classrooms as well?

**Leo:** Yes. The length of our classroom comes to nine meters, while the width is just seven.

**Ms. Rivera:** And the height?

**Leo:** About three meters, although I had to estimate it from the door frame.

**Ms. Rivera:** What about the weight of the sports equipment?

**Leo:** The football weighs around four hundred grams, though I'd like to check the shot put too.

**Ms. Rivera:** (happy) Great. Have you organized all your numbers?

**Leo:** (happy) Yes. I put them in a sequence, from smallest to largest, so the data is easier to compare.

**Ms. Rivera:** (happy) Excellent work, Leo. Your data is complete, and I'm genuinely impressed with your project.
