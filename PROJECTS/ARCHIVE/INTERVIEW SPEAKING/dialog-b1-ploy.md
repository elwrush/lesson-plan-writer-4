### MUIDS Interview — B1 (Ploy)

*Classroom practice dialog in an interview room. Two characters: an interviewer and a 14-year-old student.*

| Character | Voice notes | Key line |
|:----------|:-------------|:---------|
| **Teacher** | Authoritative adult male interviewer, professional, clear conversational delivery | "So, tell me about yourself." |
| **Ploy** | Teenage girl, slightly nervous at first, natural conversational | "Good morning. Well, my name is Ploy, and I'm fourteen." |

**Teacher:** Good morning. Please, have a seat. So, tell me about yourself.
**Ploy:** Good morning. Well, my name is Ploy, and I'm fourteen. I go to school in Bangkok. When I'm not studying, I play volleyball, and I like reading adventure books. In the future, I want to be a doctor.
**Teacher:** Nice. So why do you want to study at MUIDS?
**Ploy:** Because of the way they teach. Students learn by doing things, not just reading. For example, science students do real experiments and present them in English. I want to study medicine, so I need strong English and practical skills. So MUIDS is exactly the school I want.
**Teacher:** What's your favourite subject?
**Ploy:** Science, for sure! I love knowing how things work. For example, last term we did an experiment with magnets. So yeah, that's why science is my favourite.
**Teacher:** Another question. Should phones be banned at school?
**Ploy:** Hmm, no. I don't think phones should be banned. They're really useful for learning. For example, I can look up a word on my phone in seconds. So I think they should stay, just not during tests.
**Teacher:** Good answer. What do you do when you face a challenge?
**Ploy:** Last year, I had a really hard maths exam. I was nervous, so I made a study plan and practiced every day. In the end, I passed, and I felt so proud.
**Teacher:** Very good. Do you have any questions for us?
**Ploy:** Yes. What's a normal day at MUIDS like?
**Teacher:** We start at eight thirty, and we have five classes a day. Thank you for coming. We'll let you know our decision soon.
**Ploy:** Thank you. Goodbye.
