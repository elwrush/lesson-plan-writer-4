Should AI be allowed in classrooms?

Some schools let students use AI to help with homework. Other schools say students should think for themselves.
Structure your discussion
  1. Open: “Let's start with a big question: should AI be allowed in the classroom?”
  2. Your view: Give your opinion.
  3. Respond: Listen, then agree or disagree.
  4. Resolution: Summarise your discussion together.
Language you can use
  • I firmly believe that ...
  • There's no doubt in my mind that ...
  • That's a good point, but ...
  • I see it completely differently.
  • Could you explain why you think that?
  • To sum up, we both ...

Would school life be better without smartphones?

Before smartphones, students talked and played at break time. Now many students just look at their phones all day.
Structure your discussion
  1. Open: “Imagine a school day with no smartphones. Would it be better or worse?”
  2. Your view: Give your opinion.
  3. Respond: Listen, then agree or disagree.
  4. Resolution: Summarise your discussion together.
Language you can use
  • If I could turn back time, I would ...
  • Honestly, I think life was simpler then.
  • I can see why you'd say that, but ...
  • What do you mean exactly?
  • Let me put it another way ...
  • In the end, I'd say ...

Homework helps you practise what you learn. But too much homework can be stressful and take your free time.

  1. Open: “How much homework is too much? Let's find out.”
  2. Your view: Give your opinion.
  3. Respond: Listen, then agree or disagree.
  4. Resolution: Summarise your discussion together.
Language you can use
  • I believe it's important to ...
  • The main reason is that ...

  • How would you feel if ... ?
  • All things considered, ...
