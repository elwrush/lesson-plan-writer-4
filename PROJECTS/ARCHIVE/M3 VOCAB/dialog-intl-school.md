### Interview at International School Bangkok

*A school director interviews a teenage applicant, Lily, at International School Bangkok in Bangkok, Thailand.*

| Character | Voice notes | Key line |
|:----------|:-------------|:---------|
| **Director** | Warm, wise school director. Calm, measured, friendly. | "A good first impression matters." |
| **Lily** | Friendly, confident American teenager. | "I'm a real team player." |

**Director:** (happy) Good morning, Lily. Welcome to International School Bangkok. Please, come in.

**Lily:** (happy) Thank you. It's great to be here.

**Director:** We like to start with a handshake. (chuckling) A good first impression matters.

**Lily:** (happy) I agree. I make eye contact and smile when I meet someone new.

**Director:** Tell me about yourself. What are your best skills?

**Lily:** (thoughtful) My best skill is communication. I listen well, and I enjoy talking to new people.

**Director:** That's important. What about teamwork?

**Lily:** I'm a real team player. I like helping my group stay calm and focused.

**Director:** (curious) Are you a natural leader?

**Lily:** Sometimes. I stay calm under pressure, so people trust me with decisions.

**Director:** Have you ever had to be decisive in a difficult moment?

**Lily:** Yes. Last year, our school play lost its director. I came up with a new plan, and we acted out all the scenes in one week.

**Director:** (impressed) Wonderful. Did you ever want to give up?

**Lily:** (calm) No. Some friends dropped out of the play, but I faced up to the challenge. Hard work pays off.

**Director:** What do you want to work on?

**Lily:** My time management. I sometimes leave big tasks to the last minute.

**Director:** (warm) Patience helps. Good time management is a skill you can learn.

**Lily:** Thank you. I really hope I get a place at your school.

**Director:** (happy) You made a lasting impression today, Lily. We'll be in touch soon.

**Lily:** (happy) Thank you. I look forward to hearing from you.
