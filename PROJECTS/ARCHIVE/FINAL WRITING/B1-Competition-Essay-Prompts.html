ESSAY COMPETITION
Some students say it is easier to learn today because you can find the answer on the internet. Other students say you learn more when a teacher helps you in class.
Write an essay for the competition about learning with a teacher or learning from the internet.
Your essay must include:

which is better for you and why
Write your essay in around 100 words.
