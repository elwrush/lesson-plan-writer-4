Task 3 — Use

Instructions: Write a paragraph of about 70 words describing a difficult decision you had to make. Focus on using correct punctuation and capitalisation.
Include all of the following in your paragraph:
  • a complex sentence beginning with a subordinate conjunction (e.g., although, because, while)
  • a sentence with an appositive (e.g., my best friend, Sarah, ...)
  • correct use of a comma before a coordinating conjunction (e.g., , and or , but)
  • the word however used correctly to connect two ideas
