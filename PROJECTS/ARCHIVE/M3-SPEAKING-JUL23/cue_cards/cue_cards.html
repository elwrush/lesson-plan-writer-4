1M3 Speaking Cue Card
#1: Should we eat meat?
The question
Some people say eating animals is wrong. Other people say humans need meat to be healthy.
Structure your discussion
  1. Open: “Let's start with a question about food: Should we eat meat?”
  2. Your view: State your position. Use I think… / In my opinion…
  3. Listen & respond: I agree because… / I see it differently. / Why do you think that?
  4. Resolution: Try to summarise your discussion.
    • Great, so we both think eating less meat is a good idea!
    • Hmm, we kind of agree but not completely on this one.
    • Yikes, we really see this totally differently!
Language hints
  • Agree: Absolutely. / I think so too. / That’s a good point.
  • Disagree: I’m not sure. / I see it differently. / Yes, but…
  • Follow-up: Can you explain? / What makes you say that?
