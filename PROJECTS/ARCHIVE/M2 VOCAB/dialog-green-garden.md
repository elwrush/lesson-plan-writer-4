### The Green Garden Project

*A radio interview on "World Watch". The presenter talks to Tom, a volunteer at the Green Garden Project, a community charity in his neighbourhood.*

| Character | Voice notes | Key line |
|:----------|:-------------|:---------|
| **Presenter** | Warm, professional radio presenter. Calm, measured, friendly. | "Welcome to World Watch. Today we meet a volunteer from the Green Garden Project." |
| **Tom** | Friendly volunteer, enthusiastic and conversational. | "A neighbor encouraged me to join." |

**Presenter:** (happy) Welcome to World Watch. Today we meet a volunteer from the Green Garden Project.

**Tom:** (happy) Thanks for having me. It's great to be here.

**Presenter:** Tell us, what is the Green Garden Project?

**Tom:** It's a charity in our area. We turned an old wasteland into a garden for the community. Children play there now.

**Presenter:** (curious) How did you first get involved?

**Tom:** (friendly) A neighbor encouraged me to join. Before that, I didn't really know people here. Now I know everyone.

**Presenter:** So you got to know your community.

**Tom:** Exactly. We respect each other's rights, and everyone helps. It's a good cause.

**Presenter:** Why is a green space important for a city?

**Tom:** (thoughtful) Pollution is a big problem in cities. Green spaces give us cleaner air. They're good for our health too.

**Presenter:** Does the project help with bigger problems?

**Tom:** (calm) Yes. Some families here live in poverty. The garden gives them fresh food. It helps with hunger and gives children an education about food.

**Presenter:** (interested) You mentioned teaching. How did you learn to grow vegetables?

**Tom:** (chuckling) I taught myself! I made mistakes at first, but I didn't blame myself. You learn by trying.

**Presenter:** You're running a fundraising campaign now, right?

**Tom:** Yes. We want to buy more plants and equipment. A local company just donated a large sum of money.

**Presenter:** (warm) In a world full of conflict, this garden brings people together.

**Tom:** (happy) That's true. Everyone is welcome in the garden.

**Presenter:** How can our listeners help?

**Tom:** (happy) Come and volunteer! You can donate too. Every small effort makes a difference.

**Presenter:** (happy) Thanks, Tom. The Green Garden Project — what a great story.

**Tom:** (happy) Thank you. The garden is open to everyone.
