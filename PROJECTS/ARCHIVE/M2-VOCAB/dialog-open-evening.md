### School Open Evening

*A parent and a teacher talk at a school's open evening. Westfield High School, six o'clock in the evening.*

| Character | Voice notes | Key line |
|:----------|:-------------|:---------|
| **Ms. Clark** | Warm, welcoming teacher. Friendly and confident, with a calm measured delivery. | "We allow phones at break time. We ban them during lessons." |
| **Mr. Harris** | Polite, careful parent. Curious and thoughtful, a little formal. | "My son had problems with bullying at his old school. How do you handle that?" |

**Ms. Clark:** (happy) Good evening! Welcome to Westfield High. Can I help you?

**Mr. Harris:** Yes, I'm Mr. Harris. We're choosing a school for my son. How do you educate your students?

**Ms. Clark:** (confident) We use small classes and plenty of discussion. Every student studies the required subjects first.

**Mr. Harris:** (doubtful) How do you give grades? Do you have many exams?

**Ms. Clark:** We use continuous assessment. A student's grade comes from their work all year. They write two assignments every month.

**Mr. Harris:** What about phones? Do you allow them in class?

**Ms. Clark:** (chuckling) We allow phones at break time. We ban them during lessons.

**Mr. Harris:** My son had problems with bullying at his old school. How do you handle that?

**Ms. Clark:** (calm) We take bullying seriously. Students report it to us. We talk to everyone. We expect good behavior from all students.

**Mr. Harris:** Does every student do work experience?

**Ms. Clark:** (happy) Yes, in the final year. They spend three weeks in a real workplace.

**Mr. Harris:** (happy) That sounds good. When does the school year start?

**Ms. Clark:** (happy) In September. Bring your son to visit us. We'd love to meet him.
