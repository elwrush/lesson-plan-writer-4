Part D — The -ed Pattern

Instructions: Say each word and clap the syllables. Write the number of syllables next to the word.
1.wanted ______ syllables
2.played ______ syllables
3.stopped ______ syllables
4.needed ______ syllables
Rule: After /t/ or /d/, -ed sounds like /ɪd/ and adds one syllable: wanted = 3. After other sounds it is /t/ or /d/: played = 1, stopped = 1. Dropped finals hide the past tense — want is present, wanted is past!
