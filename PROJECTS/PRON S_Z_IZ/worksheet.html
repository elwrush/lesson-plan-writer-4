Part D — The Plural Pattern

Instructions: Say each word and clap the syllables. Write the number of syllables next to the word.
1.books ______ syllables
2.dogs ______ syllables
3.glasses ______ syllables
4.friends ______ syllables
Rule: After voiceless sounds (like /k/), -s sounds like /s/: books = 1 syllable. After voiced sounds (like /ɡ/), -s sounds like /z/: dogs = 1 syllable. After sibilants (like /s/, /z/, /ʃ/), -s sounds like /ɪz/ and adds a syllable: glasses = 2. Dropped plurals hide the meaning — book is one, books is many!
