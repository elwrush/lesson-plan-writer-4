1Interview Question 1 — Personal
Tell me about yourself.
Answer structure
  1. Who you are: My name is… I am 14 years old.
  2. What you like: I enjoy… (sports, music, reading)
  3. Your goal: In the future, I want to…
Follow-up: “What do you do in your free time?” “What is your favourite subject?”
2Interview Question 2 — Why MUIDS?
Why do you want to study at MUIDS?
Answer structure
  1. P — Position: I want to study at MUIDS because it teaches in English.
  2. R — Reason: One reason is that the school focuses on practical learning.
  3. E — Example: For example, students do experiments and projects, not just tests.
  4. P — Position: That’s why MUIDS is the right school for me.
Follow-up: “What do you know about MUIDS?” “Which subjects interest you?”
3Interview Question 3 — Favourite subject
What is your favourite subject and why?
Answer structure
  1. P — Position: My favourite subject is science.
  2. R — Reason: One reason is that I love finding out how things work.
  3. E — Example: For example, we did an experiment with magnets last term.
  4. P — Position: That’s why science is my favourite class.
Follow-up: “What do you like most about it?” “Which subject do you find difficult?”
4Interview Question 4 — Opinion
Should mobile phones be banned at school?
Answer structure
  1. P — Position: In my opinion, phones should not be banned at school.
  2. R — Reason: One reason is that phones help students learn.
  3. E — Example: For example, students can search for information quickly.
  4. P — Position: That’s why I think phones should stay at school.
Follow-up: “How much do you use your phone?” “What do you use it for?” “Do you think phone use is important?”
